Fit-Valid Smart Parking

A dimension-aware parking-allocation approach that checks whether a vehicle–space assignment is physically valid before allocation, with evaluation through simulation and an ESP32/ultrasonic prototype.

Open DOI ↗Publications
Problem

Parking availability is not the same as parking fit.

A nominally free space may still be unsuitable for a particular vehicle class. The project frames allocation around dimensional validity rather than occupancy alone.

Contribution

Hierarchical fit-valid allocation for multiple vehicle classes.

The published approach introduces a hierarchy that considers vehicle and parking-space classes before confirming an allocation.

Validation

From allocation logic to prototype evidence

The public research record supports two complementary validation modes: simulation of the allocation logic and implementation using embedded sensing hardware.

Allocation logic

Dimension-aware

Assignments are evaluated for fit validity across multiple vehicle and space classes.

Simulation

System evaluation

The allocation approach was evaluated through simulation before hardware demonstration.

Prototype

ESP32 + ultrasonic

An embedded prototype was used to demonstrate the sensing and allocation concept.
