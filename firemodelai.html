Research / FireModelAI
Computer Vision & Safety · 2025

FireModelAI

A published framework for indoor fire detection, localization and classification, treating fire awareness as an integrated perception problem rather than a binary alarm alone.

Open DOI ↗Publications
Problem

Detection is only one part of the fire-safety question.

The work addresses indoor fire awareness as a combined task: detect whether fire is present, estimate where it is, and classify the event so the system can provide richer information for response.

Contribution

An integrated detection, localization and classification framework.

FireModelAI combines visual fire detection with spatial localization and fire classification within one published system framework.

Validation

Published experimental evidence

The study reports results under its experimental conditions; the values below are presented as study-specific evidence, not universal performance claims.

Fire detection

88.46%

Reported fire-detection accuracy under the study conditions.

Localization

1.26 cm

Reported mean localization error under the study conditions.
