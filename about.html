Biography

Dr. Ahmed Ayoub

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Engineering at MSA University, Egypt, with academic and research experience across Egypt, Saudi Arabia and Hungary. My work spans intelligent sensing, embedded and edge AI, IoT and cyber-physical systems, computer vision, digital imaging and engineering education.

Research

Research Profile

My research examines how sensing, computation and physical context can be combined in intelligent systems, particularly under measurement, computing, connectivity or deployment constraints. Current areas include intelligent sensing, embedded and edge AI, IoT and cyber-physical systems, and computer vision.

Research Approach

Research Approach

I use a systems-oriented approach that keeps assumptions, constraints and evidence explicit: define the engineering question, specify the system architecture, select validation appropriate to the claim, and separate measured results from interpretation.
