Research Interests

Intelligent sensing, embedded systems and applied AI.

My research focuses on systems that combine sensing, computation and physical context. Current interests include intelligent sensing, embedded and edge AI, IoT and cyber-physical systems, computer vision, and applications in safety, agriculture and water systems.

Research Areas

Current Research Areas

Intelligent Sensing

Measurement credibility, sensor fusion and state estimation

Methods for converting imperfect measurements into useful and appropriately qualified information for monitoring and control.

Embedded & Edge AI

Intelligence under compute, power and connectivity constraints

Embedded inference, offline-first operation, resilient edge systems and edge-to-cloud architectures.

Computer Vision

Detection, localization and event understanding

Computer-vision systems for safety and monitoring applications, with emphasis on system-level validation rather than detection metrics alone.

IoT & Cyber-Physical Systems

Connected monitoring and control in real environments

IoT architectures, cyber-physical monitoring and sensing systems applied to safety, smart agriculture, water and other field environments.
